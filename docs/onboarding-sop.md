# Student Onboarding — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. Purpose

Establish a standardized procedure for onboarding newly enrolled students at a fictional educational institution.

This SOP is part of an independent portfolio simulation.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers the activities between enrollment confirmation and completion of the initial onboarding process.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Administrative Team | Verify enrollment information and coordinate onboarding |
| Onboarding Responsible Person | Track checklist completion and follow up on pending activities |
| Student | Provide required information and complete initial instructions |

These roles are defined for the simulated scenario.

## 4. Procedure

### Step 1 — Confirm Enrollment

- Verify that enrollment information has been received.
- Check whether the required student information is available.
- Identify missing or inconsistent information for follow-up.

### Step 2 — Create the Onboarding Record

- Register the student in the onboarding tracking process.
- Record the onboarding start date.
- Assign a responsible person.

### Step 3 — Send Welcome Communication

- Send the standardized welcome message.
- Provide relevant contact information.
- Explain the next onboarding steps.

### Step 4 — Share Access and Resources

- Provide instructions for accessing the student platform.
- Share relevant academic and administrative resources.
- Explain where to request support.

### Step 5 — Follow the Onboarding Checklist

- Track completion of required activities.
- Record pending items.
- Contact the student when additional information or action is required.

### Step 6 — Confirm Completion

- Verify that all essential onboarding steps have been completed.
- Record the completion status.
- Escalate unresolved issues to the appropriate responsible person.

## 5. Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Enrollment information verified.
- [ ] Onboarding record created.
- [ ] Responsible person assigned.
- [ ] Welcome communication sent.
- [ ] Access instructions shared.
- [ ] Relevant resources provided.
- [ ] Pending issues reviewed.
- [ ] Completion confirmed.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalation

If information is missing, access cannot be provided, or a student does not complete an essential step:

1. Record the pending issue.
2. Contact the student or responsible team.
3. Track the issue until resolution.
4. Update the onboarding status.

## 7. Process Completion Criteria

The onboarding process is considered complete when:

- Required enrollment information has been verified.
- Welcome communication has been sent.
- Access instructions and resources have been provided.
- Essential checklist activities have been completed.
- Outstanding issues have been recorded and addressed or formally escalated.

## 8. Disclaimer

This SOP describes a proposed procedure for a fictional educational institution. It was created for learning and portfolio purposes and does not represent an implemented procedure or actual organizational policy.
